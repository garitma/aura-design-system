# Product principles

Aura’s non-visual principles (from `.cursor/rules/principles.mdc`) guide both product and docs:

1. **Accessibility** — Perceivable, operable interfaces.
2. **Consistency** — Same voice and patterns everywhere.
3. **Reusable** — Invest once in components and tooling.
4. **Shareable** — Work should be easy to publish and adopt.
5. **User control** — People should feel in charge of the product.
6. **Forgiveness** — Undo, go back, recover from mistakes.
7. **Perception of stability** — Complex systems should feel simple and familiar.

These pair with the **visual system** in [[Foundations]] (color steps, spacing, motion, typography).
